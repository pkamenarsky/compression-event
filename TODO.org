* the speed-aware bracket
  Done, in `pinning` in `bake.ts`. The 0.3952 was never the bent seeds: the
  argmax sat one half-gap after a real event, where the truth gains the
  neighbour's own corner and that corner leaves down the neighbour's edge at
  some 7900 units per unit `t`. `abutting` drops the two pinned instants and
  closes the bracket from both sides, so each neighbour is drawn across half of
  it having been measured over none of it — and half of `GAP` at that speed is
  four tenths of a unit. The seeds only centred the bracket on the event, which
  made both sides comparable and let the grid see what `Span.worst` had been
  reporting all along.

** what changed
   `GAP` is the cap now, not the answer. An event is pinned when the outline
   travels less than `tol * MARGIN` across the half the growth will cover, and
   goes on splitting to `BEND` when it travels more.

   Measured on *one* side. Across the whole bracket sits the jump itself, and a
   jump does not shrink however far the search splits — taking the two ends
   together sent every event with any size to it down to `BEND` for nothing,
   half as much again in evaluations across the table and sixteen times on the
   level below. So it is read off the half the event is not in: `m` is the split
   that was going to happen anyway, and whichever of the two ends still compares
   with it is on the continuous side.

   Where neither end compares with `m` there is no continuous side to read, and
   that falls back to the width rule. On a level whose rings genuinely re-cut at
   every scale it would otherwise descend forever.

** what it bought
   Across the divergence table, `Span.worst`:

   | max Span.worst  | 0.3247 | -> | 0.0250 |
   | csg evaluations |   2470 | -> |   2699 |

   `nudge into a neighbour, eroding` goes 0.3247 to 0.0192 for 28 more
   evaluations. Nothing in the grid column moved, because the grid never saw any
   of this — which is why `divergence.test.ts` now fails on `Span.worst` as well
   as on its own sampling.

   On `scratch/world-2026-09-08T15-27-59Z`, 10.88 to 6.43 for 4.4x the
   evaluations. That level is the self-intersecting floor ring below and is
   still nowhere near tolerance; the gain is real but it is not the fix for it.
