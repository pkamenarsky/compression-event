* the 0.3952 is the half-gap, not the seeds
  Settled. `surd.ts` and `bentAt` in `incident.ts` solve a corner reaching an
  edge while the ring is nudged and eroded — the case `Moving` cannot model.
  Steering `seedsFor` by them took the divergence case `nudge into a neighbour,
  eroding` from `worst` 0.0193 to 0.3952, and that read as the seeds being
  wrong. They are not. They are right, and what they did was uncover a hole
  that was always there.

** what is at 0.3952
   The argmax sits at t = 0.15806, which is exactly where a stretch begins, one
   half-gap after a pinned pair of instants at 0.158012 and 0.158112. The truth
   at that instant gains a point:

   | t          | truth | replay | apart  |
   | b - 1e-6   |   5   |    5   | 0.0107 |
   | b + 1e-9   |   6   |    5   | inf    |
   | b + 1e-6   |   6   |    6   | 0.4191 |
   | b + 1e-3   |   6   |    6   | 0.3616 |

   The birth is at 0.158061604, bisected to adjacent doubles, and the point
   born is the neighbour's own corner, (150.00, 20.00). It then races down the
   neighbour's left edge: y is 20.01 at b+1e-6, 20.84 at b+1e-4, 99.58 at
   b+1e-2 — about 7900 units per unit of `t`.

   `abutting` grows the stretch after the event back over half a gap to meet
   the jump. Half of `GAP` = 1e-4 times 7900 is 0.395. That is the whole of it:
   the replay places that corner where the rings at the stretch's *measured*
   start put it, and the measured start is 5e-5 past a birth the outline leaves
   at eight thousand units a second.

** why the shipped number is 0.0193 and not this
   It is not that the shipped path avoids the defect. It is that nothing looks
   at it. With the bent seeds off the bake's own `Span.worst` on this case is
   *0.3247* — it has been saying so all along. The divergence grid steps 1e-3
   and the bad window is 1e-4 wide, so its 998 instants step straight over it;
   the two nearest land where the error has decayed to 0.0002 and 0.115.

   What the seeds change is where the window sits. Unseeded, the halving pins
   the event to a `GAP`-wide bracket somewhere off to one side, and the replay
   flips its run count late, so the instants in the bad region compare `!==`
   in run count and `apart` throws them out as infinite. Seeded, the bracket is
   centred on the event to within 4e-7, both sides are comparable, and the grid
   sees what `Span.worst` had been reporting. The seeds made the bake honest,
   and honest looked like a regression.

** so both hypotheses were wrong
   Not a pinned jump where nothing happens: the event is real, a genuine 5 to 6
   point change, and the seed named it to 4e-7. Not the halving doing double
   duty on the bend either: the error is at one instant beside one event, not
   spread over a curve.

** what it costs to fix, measured
   The seeded bracket is `s ± GAP/2` only because `GAP` was the only width
   around. A root that is isolated to 1e-8 deserves a bracket of that order.
   Sweeping the half-width `h` on this case, with the bent path on:

   | h     | stretches | csg | grid worst | Span.worst |
   | 5e-5  |        94 | 414 |     0.3952 |     0.4192 |
   | 1e-6  |       114 | 496 |     0.1822 |     0.0238 |
   | 1e-7  |       135 | 578 |     0.0200 |     0.0200 |
   | 1e-8  |       135 | 578 |     0.0200 |     0.0200 |

   `worst` is `h` times the crossing's speed, exactly. Tightening the root
   isolation to 1e-8 as well so the bracket actually straddles the event gives
   63 stretches and 295 evaluations at grid worst 0.0239 — half the shipped
   cost and an honest number.

   It is not free elsewhere. The same `h` = 1e-7 across the whole table takes
   `six boxes, turning only` from 91 stretches to 317 and `sliding through`
   from 28 to 71, and puts `nudge into a neighbour, eroding and turning` at
   0.0563, over `TOLERANCE`. A tight bracket leaves the intervals either side
   of it ending *at* the event, where the outline moves fastest, so they
   subdivide. That cost is honest, but it is a cost.

** what to do
   The knob is not `GAP` and not the solver. It is that a bracket's width has
   to be paid for at the speed the outline moves through it, and one constant
   cannot serve a corner drifting and a crossing at 7900 units per unit `t`.
   Bracket an event to `min(GAP, tol / speed)` with the speed read off the two
   ends of the bracket, and both the seeded and the halved path get a width
   they can afford. That would also take `Span.worst` on the shipped path down
   from 0.3247, which is the number that matters — it is over `TOLERANCE` today
   and has been.
